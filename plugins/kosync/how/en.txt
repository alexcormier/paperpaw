Progress sync lets a book open where you stopped on another device, also in KOReader. It uses KOReader's sync server or one of your own.

In the settings, under Progress sync, Account signs you in or creates an account, and Server sets another server. Sync automatically fetches the position when a book opens and sends it while you read, at most once a minute. Sync progress, in the reading menu under More, does it right away.

If another device is further, a question offers Go there or Stay here. Books are matched by their file, so it must be the same file on both devices.
