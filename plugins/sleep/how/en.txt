The sleep screen decides what the screen shows while the device sleeps. E-ink keeps the picture without power.

Choose it in the settings, under Display, with While asleep, show: a sleeping cat (the default), the book's cover, my pictures, the logo and "Sleeping", widgets, the page as it is, or the page with a picture. My pictures shows the files of the Pictures folder one after the other.

Without a cover or a picture the cat shows. Where the system draws the sleep screen itself, as on Android, these settings are hidden.
