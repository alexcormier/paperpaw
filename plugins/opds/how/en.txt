OPDS catalogs are online libraries such as Project Gutenberg, Calibre-Web, Kavita or Komga. This plugin browses and searches them like folders and downloads their books into the library.

Open Catalogs from the Tools menu and choose Add catalog: an address and, if the catalog needs an account, a user name and password. Tap a catalog to browse it; hold it (with buttons, hold Select) to sync, edit or remove it.

With Sync this catalog switched on, Sync catalogs downloads its new books into its folder. Books per sync limits how many one sync fetches, so a large catalog does not fill the device. A Cloud folder (WebDAV) works in place of a catalog.
