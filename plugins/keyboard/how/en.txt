The keyboard opens whenever something needs text: a search, a password, a note or a setting. It works by touch and with buttons alone.

With buttons, left and right move along a row, the page buttons move between rows, Select presses a key and Back cancels; left and right go round a row, and up and down keep to your column. Shift makes the next letter a capital; press it twice for all capitals, and once more to stop. Hold a letter (with buttons: hold Select on it) for its capital, chosen at once, and accents such as é and è; in German, ä, ö, ü and ß come so from a, o, u and s (with buttons, the keyboard says so on those letters). OK finishes. Paste inserts the clipboard text, and Phone, where offered, lets you type on your phone instead.

In the settings under Device, Keyboard: Keyboard layouts chooses the layouts. By default it follows the menu language; with several, a key switches between them. Umlaut keys gives ä, ö, ü and ß keys of their own instead. Size makes keys and letters larger; with buttons alone, the rows stay those of a computer keyboard. On touch screens, Key shape changes how the keys look. Automatic capitals starts each sentence with a capital.
