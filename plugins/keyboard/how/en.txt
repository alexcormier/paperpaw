The keyboard opens whenever something needs text: a search, a password, a note or a setting. It works by touch and with buttons alone.

With buttons, left and right move along a row, the page buttons move between rows, Select presses a key and Back cancels. Hold a letter (with buttons: hold Select on it) for accents such as é and è. OK finishes. Paste inserts the clipboard text, and Phone, where offered, lets you type on your phone instead.

Under System in the settings, Keyboard layouts chooses the layouts. By default it follows the menu language; with several, a key switches between them.
