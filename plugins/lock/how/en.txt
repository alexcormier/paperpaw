The screen lock asks for a PIN, a pattern or a password when PaperPaw starts and when the device wakes up. It is off at first. It keeps others from casual use, not someone who takes the files.

Set it in the settings, under Device, Screen lock: enter the code in use if there is one, choose the kind, then the new code twice. A pattern is offered on touch screens only.

After five wrong tries the next one waits half a minute. Lock now locks at once and can be put on a button, a gesture or a rule. Lock when waking up can be switched off, except where every wake is a start, as on the X4 on battery.

Touch lock, beside it: Lock touch in the quick panel and the reading menu makes taps, swipes and the pen do nothing while the buttons still work, e.g. to read with a hand on the screen. To unlock, tap twice with one finger and keep it down a moment; on the X4 Pro, holding the power button does it too.
