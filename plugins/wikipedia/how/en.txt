Wikipedia looks words up online, beside the dictionaries. When you look up a word in a book, the dictionary panel lists what your dictionaries find. Choose Online to ask Wikipedia too: the short summary of its article appears as one more entry, in the language of the book, else of PaperPaw.

Open the entry to read it on a screen of its own. The page buttons or taps scroll it, and Select or a tap at its end closes it.

It needs a network. If there is no article, it says so.

The summaries come from Wikipedia under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (CC BY-SA 4.0); each names its article below it.
