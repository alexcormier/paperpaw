Settings shows everything that can be set in PaperPaw and its plugins, in pages. Plugins need no screen of their own: what they declare appears here, in groups, with each setting's description below it.

Open it with Settings in the menu. Tap a group to open it, then a setting to switch or choose it; numbers have − and + buttons. With buttons, move to a setting and press Select: left and right change a number, Select keeps it, Back returns to the earlier value.

Changes apply at once. Search finds a setting by words and Advanced settings adds the rarely needed ones. Reset to defaults and Reset all ask first.
