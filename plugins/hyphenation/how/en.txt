Hyphenation breaks long words at the end of a line where they may be broken. The text looks more even, with fewer wide gaps. This plugin brings the patterns for German (reformed spelling) and English (American); words are broken in the book's language.

Hyphenation is on at first. Switch it off or on in the settings, under Reading, with Hyphenate words. The patterns take almost no memory. Other languages come as plugins of their own.
