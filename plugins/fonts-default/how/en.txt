Default fonts brings the fonts PaperPaw comes with: Literata for reading books, with regular, bold, italic and bold italic, and Source Sans 3 for menus and lists. It also supplies the icons of the menus.

Literata is what books are set in unless you choose another font in Settings, under Reading, as Font in the Text section; a book that brings its own fonts can use them too. Source Sans is used for everything outside the book. The licences of these fonts are in About PaperPaw, under Licences.

To read with other fonts, copy .ttf or .otf files into the fonts folder, or download some with More fonts.
