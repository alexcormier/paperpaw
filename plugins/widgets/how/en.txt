The widget pack adds many widgets for the home screen: clocks (analogue, word, tiles, world), a calendar, the year so far, a countdown, the moon phase, and day and date; a quote of the day, notes, to-dos, pictures, a greeting and the PaperPaw logo; and for books, now reading, progress, next up, series, favourites, a highlight and the library in numbers. A battery widget is in it too.

Add them with Edit home screen in the menu (or a long press on the home screen), then Add a widget. Each has settings of its own, such as frame, size and alignment. Choosing a widget that changes, such as the quote of the day, shows the next one. Your own quotes go into quotes.txt in .paperpaw/plugins/widgets on the card, each line as text | author. The time widgets need the clock set; Wi-Fi sets it.
