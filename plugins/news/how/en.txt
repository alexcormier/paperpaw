News turns RSS or Atom feeds into books. Each feed becomes one book in the News folder of the library, with its newest articles as plain text, to read like any other book.

Open News from the Tools menu and choose Add a feed; its address is on the website of the newspaper or blog. Fetch the news fetches all feeds and replaces the earlier books. Hold a feed (with buttons, hold Select) to fetch or remove it alone. A feed removed leaves its book in the library.

Fetching needs a network. The setting News articles a feed sets how many articles each book holds. The News headlines widget shows them on the home screen.
