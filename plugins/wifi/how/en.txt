Wi-Fi joins networks when something needs one and switches the radio off again, to save battery and memory. It applies where PaperPaw manages Wi-Fi itself, such as the X4; elsewhere the system does it.

The radio is off by default. It comes on when a plugin wants the network, joins the strongest saved network and goes off a minute after the last use (Switch off after changes that). Open the Wi-Fi page in the settings, or tap the Wi-Fi sign in the status line, to choose a network and enter its password. The menu button or a long press forgets a saved network.

Set up with a phone lets you pick the network and type its password on a phone.
