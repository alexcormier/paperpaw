The menu lists what you can do and set. It opens at the bottom of the screen, above whatever is open: while reading, tap the middle of the screen or press Select. Tap or select an entry to run it.

Settings in the menu show their value and change in place, so the menu stays open. An entry with more entries opens a submenu.

Swipe down from the top edge for the quick panel: what you change often, as buttons. Tap a button to switch it, hold it for its settings. With buttons only, the arrow buttons choose, Select switches and Back closes. Under Appearance, Menus shows menus as a list or as tiles.
