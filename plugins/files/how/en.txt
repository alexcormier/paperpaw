The library lists the books in your library folder, each with its cover, title, author and how far you have read it. The book you read last stands on top as a card, so going on reading takes one tap, or one press of Select.

Choose what is shown above the books: all of them, those you are reading, unread or finished ones, or the shelves (by author, series, tag, favourites). Tap a book to read it; hold it (with buttons, hold Select) for its information, favourites, moving it to the archive or deleting it.

The library folder is set in Settings, under Library, as Library folder, and there you can also show books as a grid of covers. For every other file, use Files in the Tools menu.
