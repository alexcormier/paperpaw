Wallabag fetches the articles you saved in Wallabag as books, with their pictures, into the folder Wallabag of your library. It is off at first: add it with More plugins, or switch it on under Plugins if it is listed there.

Open Wallabag from Tools. Enter the server, your user name and password, and the ID and secret of an API client made in Wallabag under API clients management. Then choose Fetch articles, with Wi-Fi on.

Articles you read to the end are archived in Wallabag with the next fetch, and their books are removed. A book in which you made highlights, notes or bookmarks stays in your library with them. If you switch to another server or account, the books fetched before stay too.
