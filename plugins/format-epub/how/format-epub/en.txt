EPUB books open from the library like any other book, with their table of contents, images and the publisher's styling. Web pages (HTML), Markdown, FictionBook (.fb2) and comic book archives (.cbz) open the same way, each as a plugin of its own.

A chapter is converted the first time you open it and kept in the plugin's cache, so it is quicker afterwards and large books work on small devices. Find the table of contents under Contents in the reading menu. Whether the book's own styling applies is the setting Publisher's formatting, under Reading.
