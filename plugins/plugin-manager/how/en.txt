The plugin manager lists every plugin with its version and description, and switches plugins on and off.

Find it in Settings, as Manage plugins. Plugin settings is a separate page for preferences such as each plugin's network use. Tap a plugin to switch it on or off (with buttons, select it). The change applies after a restart: the bar at the bottom then offers Restart now. Hold a plugin (with buttons, hold Select) to read how it works.

A plugin marked Always on cannot be switched off, and one marked Required only once another plugin does its job, or PaperPaw would have no way back. Under Screens you choose which plugin shows a place that several offer.
