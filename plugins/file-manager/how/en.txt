The file manager shows every folder and file on the device's storage, not only your books, and lets you open, rename, move, copy and delete them and make new folders.

Open Files from the Tools menu. Tap a folder to go into it; Back goes to the folder above. Tap a file, or hold any entry (with buttons, hold Select), to see what can be done with it. Move and Copy pick the entry: go to the folder it belongs in and choose Paste here in the bar at the bottom. With buttons, press Down from the last entry to reach the bar.

Files and folders starting with a dot, such as PaperPaw's own data, are hidden. To see them, switch on Show hidden files in Settings, under System.
