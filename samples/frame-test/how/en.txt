Frame test shows a moving pattern at a fixed pace and does a fixed amount of work for every frame, as a game or an emulator would. It says how many frames a second the screen shows, how long the work took as hot code and as ordinary code, how long drawing the pattern took, and how much memory is free.

Left and right move the square. Up and down double or halve the work. Confirm switches between hot and ordinary code. Next page cleans the screen. Back leaves; holding Back for two seconds always leaves.
